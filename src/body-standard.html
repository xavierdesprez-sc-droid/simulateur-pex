Formulas & Economic Model (Native KaTeX Formulas)

1. 20% floor — no decrease (≥ 20%)

The new bonus share is defined by the rule: \(\text{Share}_{\text{new}} = \max(20\%, \text{Share}_{\text{current}})\) A rep at \(25\%\) stays at \(25\%\), while a rep at \(10\%\) or \(17\%\) automatically moves to \(20\%\).

2. Weighted Curve & Purple Point

The frame automatically adapts its scale to show the full weighted payout: \(\text{Payout}_{\text{weighted}} = \text{Payout}_{\text{new}}(x) \times \frac{\text{Nominal}_{\text{new}}}{\text{Nominal}_{\text{old}}}\) Even for small initial bonuses, the curve remains fully visible.

3. Synchronization & Global Corridor

The additional corridor gain is computed as: \(\text{Gain C2P} = \text{C2P Total} \times \Delta_{\text{perf}} \times \text{Corridor}\) with \(700\text{ M€} \times 1\% \times 20\% = 1.4\text{ M€}\) of additional gross C2P per point of overperformance.
