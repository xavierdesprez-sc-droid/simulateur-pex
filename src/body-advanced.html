Hybrid Scheme: nominal transition between 85% and 100%

Below 0% achievement: 0 paid. From 0% to 85%: old scheme (linear on the old nominal). From 85% to 100%: linear transition from the old curve to the new one. Beyond: new curve — linear from 0 to 100% up to the new nominal, then linear from 100% to 200%. New nominal = max(20% target, € minimum nominal, existing nominal kept) • Value at 200% = max(2× new nominal, € minimum amount). Each rep has their own curve: the lower their old nominal, the bigger the jump between the transition bounds. Without the adjustment (and when no guaranteed floors bind), a rep already at ≥ 20% has a ratio of 1: their hybrid is identical to the old scheme. When enabled, the old curve and the start of the transition are evaluated at achievement plus the target increase.
